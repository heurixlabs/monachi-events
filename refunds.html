Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

If you cancel

  • 48 hours or more before the event: full refund.
  • Less than 48 hours before the event: no refund, but you can transfer the pass to another child for the same event, or ask to move it to another MONACHI event (subject to availability).
  • No-show: no refund.

If we cancel or reschedule

If MONACHI cancels an event, you get a full refund. If we change the date, you can keep your booking for the new date or ask for a full refund.

How to cancel

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 72170 54807 with your booking code (for example MON-HW-XXXXXX).

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days after we approve them. Banks may take a little longer to show the amount.
