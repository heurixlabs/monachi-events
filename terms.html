About us

MONACHI ("Crafting Celebrations") runs craft workshops, themed kids' parties and festive events in Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India. Contact details are on our Contact Us page.

Bookings and prices

  • Prices are shown in Indian Rupees (₹) per child, including all charges. The amount you pay is shown before payment.
  • A booking is confirmed only after full payment. You will receive a Party Pass for each child.
  • Please enter correct details. The child's and parent's names are printed on the pass.
  • Each event has an age range. Please book only for children within that range.

Payments

Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking). We never see or store your card or bank details.

Delivery of passes

There is no physical delivery. Your Party Pass is shown on screen straight after payment, can be downloaded as a PDF, and is emailed to the address you give. If you can't find it, use "My pass" on our website or message us on WhatsApp.

At the event

  • Please show the Party Pass at the entry. One pass admits one child.
  • Tell us about allergies or special needs when booking.
  • Parents or guardians remain responsible for their children before and after the event times, and for any personal belongings.
  • We may take photos of activities and creations. We do not publish children's faces without the parent's permission.
  • We may change activities, timings or the venue for reasons beyond our control. We will tell you as early as possible.
