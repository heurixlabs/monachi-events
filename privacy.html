Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you book or enquire with MONACHI, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Parent's or guardian's name, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Each child's name and age, and any notes you add (for example allergies).
  • For enquiries: your preferred date, number of guests and message.
  • Payment status and reference numbers from Razorpay. We do not receive or store card, UPI PIN or bank details.

How we use it

  • To confirm bookings, create Party Passes and let you in at the event.
  • To contact you about your booking, changes to the event, or your enquiry.
  • To tell you when booking opens for an event, if you asked us to ("Notify me").

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the services that run our bookings: Razorpay (payments) and Google (where booking records are stored and emails are sent). We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed for the event, accounts and legal requirements, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to stop messages, at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 72170 54807.
